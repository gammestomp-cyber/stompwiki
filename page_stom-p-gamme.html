Stom P. Gamme

Stom P. Gamme was a royal scientist from 1000 years before the 1st stomping. He comes from a long line of inventors and had made scrapped plans for a defective TIME MACHINE and before he disappeared, he made a shrink ray. He was planning on making the shrink ray shrink multiple things at once, but on accident he made the entire species of ants and mice tiny. He stayed big, but he was filled with guilt. He was not seen again.
